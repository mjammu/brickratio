# Voice & copy rules
> Read before writing or rewriting any visible copy.

## TONE OF VOICE

Write like a calm, senior person who understands small business owners. Empathetic, plain, specific.

**Headlines (Mahesh's standing rule):**
- Use a **question** or a **calm statement**. Never a command, and never an imperative tail ("Get it out.", "Lift it.", "Pick how much…").
- Never make the reader feel judged or bad.
- Italic fade on the emotional phrase: `<em className="om-em">…</em>`.

**Always:**
- Lead with outcomes, not features.
- Plain language a non-technical owner understands. Explain jargon ("prompts" = copy-and-send messages; "systems" = a repeated task written step by step).
- Be specific. Vague claims kill trust.
- Keep sections short. Mahesh wants the page simple and easy to digest; push back on over-engineering.

**Never:**
- Em dashes (—) in visible copy. Use commas, colons or periods.
- "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "next-generation", "state-of-the-art", "harness the power of AI".
- Exclamation points (exception: inside quoted example output, like the onboarding demo).
- Passive voice, hype, or claims we can't back up. No made-up stats; no client names without permission.
- A period after "Brick Ratio" in the logo.
