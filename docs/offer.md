## THE OFFER

**Name:** The Owner's Manual Method. Always "Owner's Manual Method" when naming the offer. It's a *method*, not just a document. "Owner's Manual" alone refers to the *deliverable* (the written manual a customer ends up with).

**What it is:** A guided way to get how a business runs into writing, so AI can work from it.
1. **You talk.** Your AI interviews you, one question at a time. Speak or type.
2. **AI writes your manual.** A plain-language manual: your voice, offers, pricing and processes, with gaps flagged.
3. **AI works from it.** Any AI can use it to handle onboarding, client replies, proposals and follow-ups the way the owner would.

**The pain:** The owner's brain is the limiting factor. Everything in the business runs through their head, even if they have documents and SOPs. That caps bandwidth and growth.

**The promise:** The business gets put into words, so AI can work from it. The business is no longer limited to what the owner's brain can hold or remember.

**Who it's for:** Solo owners of service businesses (coaches, consultants, agencies, freelancers, solo practitioners, one-person studios). No technical skills needed.

**Tools the customer needs:**
- Required: one paid AI plan. **Claude Pro** (preferred) or **ChatGPT Plus** (also works).
- Optional: **Wispr Flow**, to talk instead of type.
- (Don't list "a computer." It's assumed.)

**Portability:** The manual is plain files the customer owns. Switching AI tools keeps the business intact. (Mentioned in the FAQ, not as its own section.)
